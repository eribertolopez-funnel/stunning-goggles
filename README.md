# Stunning Goggles

The stunning-goggles factory is where Gru's minions get their signature eyewear made. Each pair is built to survive a minion's typical workday — chemical spills, banana-related mishaps, and the occasional stray laser blast.

## What we make

- **Stunning Goggles** — the classic round-lens design minions are known for, delivering top-tier eye protection with undeniable style.
- Durable, stretchy straps sized to fit snugly over a minion's head, regardless of how many eyes it has.
- Scratch-resistant lenses tough enough to handle lab accidents and evil-scheme mishaps alike.

## How it works

Raw materials enter one end of the factory, and rows of minions on the assembly line shape, polish, and strap together each pair by hand. Every finished pair is quality-checked for that unmistakable stunning shine before shipping out to minions everywhere.
