# Stunning Goggles Factory

Deep in the heart of Minion HQ, the Stunning Goggles Factory designs and builds the iconic eyewear every minion relies on for their daily mischief. Each pair of stunning goggles is engineered to survive explosions, banana spills, and the occasional botched invention gone wrong.

## What we make

- **Stunning Goggles** — the signature round, silver-rimmed goggles that keep every minion looking sharp and ready for action.
- **Shock-resistant lenses** — built to handle lab accidents, fireworks, and Gru's latest experiments.
- **One-size-fits-most straps** — because minions come in a delightful variety of shapes and heights.
- **Banana-proof coating** — a factory specialty, keeping lenses clear no matter how much banana ends up nearby.

## Gru

Every great factory needs a mastermind, and this one belongs to Gru. As founder and resident supervillain-turned-family-man, Gru personally signs off on every goggle design, insisting on just the right balance of style and shock-resistance for whatever scheme is currently in motion. He commissions new goggle models whenever his plans call for it — moon heists, rival takedowns, or simply keeping his minions safe from their own chaos — and drops by the factory floor often enough that the minions have learned to keep a spare pair of goggles on hand, just in case he wants to try them on himself.

## How it works

The factory floor runs a simple pipeline: raw materials in, goggles out. Minions on the assembly line inspect, polish, and pack each pair before they ship off to be worn on the next big adventure — evil or otherwise.
