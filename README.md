# Stunning Goggles Factory

The Stunning Goggles Factory manufactures protective eyewear built specifically for minions. Every pair of stunning goggles rolls off the same line, engineered to keep minion eyes safe while they get up to their usual chaos.

## What we make

Stunning goggles are the signature safety gear of the minion world — round, snug-fitting, and tough enough to survive lab explosions, banana-related mishaps, and the occasional supervillain scheme gone sideways.

## Key features

- **Impact-resistant lenses** — shrug off sparks, debris, and stray gadgets
- **Snug elastic strap** — stays on no matter how much jumping, running, or gibberish shouting is involved
- **One-size-fits-most** — built to match the classic minion look
- **Quick-clean design** — because minions are not known for keeping things tidy

Every pair leaves the factory ready to help a minion see clearly, work safely, and look the part.
