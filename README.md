# Stunning Goggles Factory

The Stunning Goggles Factory designs and builds stunning goggles, the signature protective eyewear worn by minions on missions for their employer. The factory turns raw materials into finished goggles through a repeatable production line, ensuring every pair meets the standards minions rely on in the field.

## Factory Layout

The factory floor is organized into a straight-line production flow:

- **Raw materials intake** where lens blanks, frame stock, and strap materials arrive and are inspected
- **Lens grinding and shaping station** where raw lens blanks are ground and polished into their final shape
- **Frame assembly line** where frames are molded and fitted around the finished lenses
- **Strap fitting station** where adjustable straps are attached and sized
- **Quality-control checkpoint** where every pair is inspected for durability and fit before moving on
- **Packaging and shipping dock** where approved goggles are boxed and sent out to minions

## The Goggles

Stunning goggles are built for minions who need reliable eye protection in chaotic, high-energy environments:

- **Impact-resistant lenses** that shield eyes from debris, explosions, and lab mishaps
- **Adjustable strap** for a snug fit across the wide range of minion head sizes
- **Signature round lens design** instantly recognizable as minion gear, also available in square, cat-eye, and aviator-style shapes so minions can pick the fit and look they prefer
- **Durable frame** built to survive drops, scrapes, and the general chaos of minion life

Whether a minion is assisting with an evil scheme or just goofing around, stunning goggles keep them looking sharp and seeing clearly.
