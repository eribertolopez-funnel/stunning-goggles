# Stunning Goggles

The stunning-goggles factory is where Gru's minions get their signature eyewear designed, assembled, and quality-checked before heading out into the field. Every pair rolls off the line ready to handle the chaos of minion life, from banana-hoarding schemes to the occasional trip through a jelly-filled donut.

## What are Stunning Goggles?

Stunning Goggles are the standard-issue eyewear worn by minions on duty. They combine rugged protection with a bit of mischievous flair, keeping every minion's single (or double) eye safe and stylish no matter the mission.

## Key Features

- **Impact-resistant lenses** — built to survive drops, explosions, and enthusiastic goggle-swapping
- **Snug elastic strap** — one-size-fits-most, adjustable for minions of any height
- **Anti-fog coating** — stays clear through banana-peeling steam and secret-lab smoke
- **Iconic round frame** — the unmistakable look every minion is known for
- **Easy-clean design** — wipes off grape soda, jelly, and other minion mishaps in seconds

## Why Minions Need Them

Whether they're storming a vault, operating heavy machinery, or just goofing around, minions rely on Stunning Goggles to keep their eyes protected and their vision sharp. The stunning-goggles factory keeps production running around the clock so no minion ever has to go on a mission without a fresh pair.
